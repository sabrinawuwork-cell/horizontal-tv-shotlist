# NORTH EXIT — Episode 1 excerpt

Shooting language: English (en-US)

## SCENE 1 — INT. ROADSIDE DINER — NIGHT

Rain tracks down the windows. NORA sits alone in a booth with an unopened envelope beside her coffee. ETHAN enters, sees the envelope, and slides into the seat opposite her. MAYA, the night waitress, approaches with a pot of coffee.

ETHAN: You shouldn't have brought that here.

NORA keeps one hand on the envelope.

NORA: You said it was destroyed.

MAYA recognizes the seal on the envelope. She stops pouring before the cup is full.

MAYA: My father used that seal.

ETHAN reaches for the envelope. NORA pulls it back. MAYA sets the pot down between them.

ETHAN: Maya, walk away.

MAYA: Not this time.

NORA opens the envelope. Inside is a photograph of ETHAN beside MAYA'S father at the north exit of the old mine.

NORA: Then tell us why you were there.

Ethan looks toward the diner door instead of answering.

## SCENE 2 — EXT. DINER PARKING LOT — NIGHT

The rain has weakened. Ethan reaches his parked sedan. Nora follows with the photograph inside her coat. Maya remains under the diner awning, visible through the window behind them.

ETHAN unlocks the driver's door.

ETHAN: Get in. I'll explain on the road.

NORA: You're not choosing the road anymore.

Nora holds up the photograph. Ethan stops with the door half open. Across the lot, a second car turns on its headlights but does not move. Ethan sees it first.

ETHAN: Put that away.

Nora follows his eyeline to the second car. The headlights go dark.
